# CompMechSelfStudy
Repository to freely publish and share a series of books I have written 
on computational mechanics, focused on self-study. The works are 
dedicated to principles, mathematical, formulation, numerical modeling, 
and programming.

# Objective
This series contains material on the mathematical formulation of continuum 
mechanics, and python programming. We believe that the new and young 
generation of reasearchers in computational mechanics need a solid base 
to quickly progress on their knowledge of principles and skills, which 
invariably underlie all aspects of their career. 

This series is focused on self-study, hence the reader can use it as a 
gentle guide to teach him or herself mathematical formulation, numerical 
modeling, and programming. This series is inspired by the book Mathematics 
for Self-Study, by J. E. Thompson, the same book used by Richard Feynman 
to teach himself calculus during his teens. 

This series contains exercises and challenges sprinkled all over the text. 
Consequently, the reader can learn and practice as the reading progresses.
